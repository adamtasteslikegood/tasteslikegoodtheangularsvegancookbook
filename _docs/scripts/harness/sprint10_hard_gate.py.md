## Overview

This module implements the Sprint 10 "hard gate" check used by the harness to verify that a sprint and its committed items meet the sprint-closing rules. It is a standalone, read-only command-line script that runs rules 1, 2 and 4 from the Sprint 10 gate specification (it skips rule 3 when invoked with --charter), verifies sprint existence and status, ensures every non-droppable committed item is a sprint member, and confirms that each committed SI has a rendered RCP acceptance row on the board. The script uses an internal Jira client for board and sprint queries and accepts flags such as --charter, --issues, and --json to alter its behavior and output format.
