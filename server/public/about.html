The anti-recipe site

Most recipe sites make you scroll past pop-ups, autoplay video and a life story before you reach the ingredients. TastesLikeGood does the opposite: the recipe comes first, with one photo of the finished dish and nothing in the way. There are no ads, no advertising cookies and no paywall.

What it does

VeganGenius Chef is a free AI vegan recipe generator. Describe a dish, a craving or the ingredients in your fridge, and it writes a complete plant-based recipe with Google Gemini: measured ingredients, a step-by-step method, prep and cook times and servings. An AI image model then makes a photo of the finished dish.

You can save recipes to your own cookbook, as a guest in your browser or across devices when you sign in with Google, and publish the ones you are proud of to the public recipe catalog.

About the recipes

Every recipe is generated to be vegan: no meat, fish, dairy or eggs. They are AI-written, so read them the way you would read a recipe from a friend: check the labels on packaged ingredients for allergens, and trust your own judgement at the stove. Nutrition figures are not calculated.

Who makes it
