# Research Results: TastesLikeGood.org

## Site Overview

- **Domain**: tasteslikegood.org
- **Primary Offer**: Free vegan AI recipe generator + browsable recipe library
- **Key Differentiators**:
  - Vegan-only recipes, no ads, no paywall
  - Ingredient/craving-based recipe generation
  - Recipe saving to personal cookbook
  - Low-clutter experience
  - Generated recipes include AI food photography
- **Homepage**: Generates 104 published recipes (per sitemap)
- **Structured Data**: WebApplication, WebSite, and FAQPage schemas present
- **Robots.txt**: Allows general crawling, disallows `/api/` except public recipe images, lists sitemap
- **Sitemap**: Lists homepage, browse/about pages, 12 category pages, and recipe pages

## SERP Findings (with important caveats)

**Critical limitation**: Google blocked the search request; Bing results were inconsistent or irrelevant for target phrase "vegan recipe generator." Cannot reliably report current rankings, SERP features, keyword volume, or difficulty from this session. The keyword opportunities below are hypotheses to validate in Search Console or a reliable SEO tool.

### What was verified on-site:

- **Title/H1/primary copy** use the core phrase "vegan recipe generator"
- **Homepage** prominently displays the free/no-ads/no-paywall proposition
- **Generator** section: "Type what you're craving and get a complete vegan recipe in seconds"
- **About page**: Clear disclosure that recipes are AI-generated; warns readers to check ingredient labels on packaged ingredients for allergens
- **Recipe pages**: Include ingredients (WET/DRY categories), quantities, method, prep/cook times, servings, chef's notes, tags (vegan, chinese, sichuan, etc.)
- **Category pages**: High-protein, gluten-free, breakfast, lunch, dinner, dessert, tofu, etc.
- **Sitemap**: Contains 12 category URLs + homepage/about browse URLs
- **Content type**: AI-generated recipes with AI-generated food photography

### SERP reality check:

- The phrase "vegan recipe generator" has clear tool-seeking intent
- TastesLikeGood is well aligned with that intent
- Visible differentiators: vegan-only, ingredient-based prompts, recipe saving, low-clutter experience
- Competitive landscape includes other recipe-generation tools, broader AI cooking apps, pantry-matching sites
- SERP data was too unreliable to name definitive rankings or claim competitive gaps based on positions

## SEO Priorities

1. **Above-the-fold value proposition**: Show concrete example prompts and sample outputs for:
   - "What can I make with tofu, broccoli, peanut butter?"
   - "Quick 15-minute vegan dinner"
   - "Dinner party vegan main course"
     Keep primary CTA obvious.

2. **Build distinct category pages** around needs already reflected in the site's navigation:
   - High-protein vegan recipes
   - Gluten-free vegan recipes
   - Vegan tofu recipes
   - Quick dinners (under 30 minutes)
   - Vegan breakfasts
   - Vegan desserts
   - Avoid near-duplicate pages made just to target keyword variants

3. **Strengthen recipe quality and trust**:
   - Transparent AI generation disclosure (already present)
   - Add clear explanation of how recipes are checked/quality-assured
   - Only use author/reviewer details where they accurately reflect real people and review work
   - Don't imply recipes are certified safe for allergies or celiac disease unless truly verified

4. **Curate the recipe catalogue before expanding**:
   - Some sitemap titles appear unusually themed/technical for a recipe site
   - Improve anything that would disappoint someone searching for a practical recipe
   - Verify recipe structured data on live recipe URLs using Google's Rich Results Test

5. **Verify recipe structured data**: Homepage's WebApplication markup does not establish that recipe pages have valid Recipe markup. Only mark up visible, accurate content; don't invent ratings or nutrition values.

6. **Use Search Console to confirm indexing and identify impressions, clicks, and queries**:
   - Sitemap presence and crawlable robots rules are helpful
   - Don't prove that pages are indexed or ranking on their own

## Blog Audience and Topic Ideas

1. **Busy new vegans and flexitarians**: Practical, approachable meals and substitutions
2. **Vegan home cooks planning around what's already in the fridge or pantry**
3. **Cooks seeking particular recipe types**: tofu dinners, vegan comfort food, gluten-free options
4. **Food creators curious about AI recipe tools and their real-world usefulness**

### Good first article tests:

- "What can I make with tofu, broccoli, and peanut butter? Vegan dinner ideas"
- "High-protein vegan dinners built around lentils, tofu, and tempeh"
- "Vegan pantry substitutions: what to use when you're missing an ingredient"
- "We tested AI-generated vegan recipes in a real kitchen" — publish only with documented human cooking and evaluation
- "Gluten-free vegan cooking: ingredient labels and cross-contact checks"

## Backlink Approach

Prioritize assets and relationships that give other sites a reason to cite you:

1. **Documented hands-on comparison of vegan recipe tools** — test 2-3 tools, publish results with photos/notes
2. **Genuinely useful vegan substitution guide** — ingredient alternatives with practical notes
3. **Original test of recipes cooked by people** — not just AI generation claims
4. **Pitch to**: vegan food bloggers, recipe newsletters, vegan organizations, cooking educators
5. **Specific contribution** (not generic link request)

### Helpful directory listings:

- General recipe sites, vegan resource pages, cooking tool directories

### Avoid:

- Paid-link schemes
- Mass guest-post campaigns with no editorial control
- Reciprocal-link networks
- Linking from low-quality or irrelevant directories

## Recommendations Summary

1. **Immediate on-page fixes**:
   - Add example prompt/output sections above the fold on the generator homepage
   - Ensure category pages have unique, useful descriptions (not just tag lists)
   - Verify all recipe structured data with Google's Rich Results Test
   - Add clear authorship/attribution where applicable and accurate

2. **Content strategy**:
   - Start with 3-5 high-quality blog articles targeting the audience ideas above
   - Link each article to relevant category pages and specific recipes
   - Include the generator link where it genuinely helps the reader
   - Follow up with Search Console data before creating more content

3. **Link building**:
   - Create 1-2 definitive assets (comparison guide, substitution guide, hands-on test)
   - Pitch 10-15 relevant vegan/plant-based sites with specific contribution offers
   - Claim useful directory listings for discovery
   - Monitor brand mentions and convert unlinked mentions to links

4. **Measurement**:
   - Set up Search Console tracking for key queries
   - Track impressions/clicks for new blog articles
   - Monitor referral traffic from any link building efforts
   - Adjust strategy based on actual data, not assumed rankings
